5. Maintenance Authority, Emergency Action, 20% Out-of-Scope Coordination Markup & 15% Tier Shared Calendar Rule

To maintain five-star guest habitability and prevent reservation cancellations, Manager is granted pre-authorized spending authority up to $250.00 per incident for non-structural maintenance, emergency plumbing, lock servicing, or appliance resets. Repairs exceeding $250.00 require Owner electronic approval, except in cases of immediate emergency (active water leaks, fire hazards, or security lockouts) where failure to act would result in property damage or platform penalty.

Transparent 20% Coordination Markup on Extra Out-of-Scope Services: While routine turnover cleaning passes through 100% to the cleaning team with zero markup, for extra services coordinated by Manager outside typical management charges (including coordinating third-party repairs and maintenance dispatches, sourcing and installing replacement household items, or packaging and mailing lost-and-found items back to departed guests), Manager applies a transparent twenty percent (20.0%) coordination markup on the underlying vendor invoice or shipping/replacement cost so out-of-scope operational labor is fairly compensated.

15% Essential Tier Shared Calendar & Maintenance Responsibility: When Owner elects the 15% Essential Tier, Manager actively manages dynamic pricing, promotions, and calendar strategy while Owner coordinates directly with cleaners and maintenance vendors; accordingly, calendar blocks and turnover timing operate as a shared responsibility between Owner and Manager, and any out-of-scope emergency repair or guest item recovery requested of Manager at the 15% tier is billed at actual cost plus the 20.0% coordination markup.

6. Direct & Agentic Bookings (+6.0% Channel Fee), Damage Waivers & Resolution Center 50% Split Rule

Direct & Agentic Channel Bookings: Direct Bookings and Agentic AI Marketplace bookings carry the same base management commission percentage as the property's contracted tier (whether 15.0%, 20.0%, or 25.0%), plus an additional 6.0% Direct & Agentic Channel Fee to cover merchant credit card interchange (~3.0%) and direct booking infrastructure and operational costs that Manager does not absorb (saving Owner substantially compared to 14.0% to 15.5% OTA host and guest service fees). In addition, because there is no OTA AirCover on Direct Bookings or agentic bookings, Manager shall collect the prevailing Guest Damage Protection Waiver fee at the time of the stay (folded directly into the total cost of the stay paid by the guest) upon Owner sign-off for Direct Booking distribution.

Resolution Center & Damage Claim 50% Split (Invoice Verification Requirement): In the event Owner requests Manager to file, document, or manage a platform Resolution Center, AirCover, or damage claim, Manager shall retain fifty percent (50.0%) of the claim payout UNLESS a verified, paid invoice or receipt is provided proving actual out-of-pocket replacement, repair, or third-party vendor disbursement. Specifically: (a) if a claim is filed for extra turnover cleaning (e.g. a $250.00 excessive mess fee), those funds must be remitted directly to the cleaning team upon receipt of their invoice rather than retained by Owner; and (b) for example, if a claim is filed for a damaged chair that is fixed without paying for an external repair, or a claim is requested for a broken item such as a plant pot that is not replaced or repaired (without a paid replacement or repair invoice), the recovery payout is split 50/50 between Owner and Manager to compensate for the substantial administrative labor, evidence compilation, and guest/platform negotiation required.
