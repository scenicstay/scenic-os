Cleaner Vetting, Visual Interview & 5-Turn Trial SOP

How to vet cleaners before handing them keys: practical "spot-the-flaw" tests, AirCover damage traps, and a probationary 5-turn gauntlet.

STEP 1 (15 MIN) Phone Screen

The STR vs Residential Filter

Filter out residential cleaners who lack STR urgency. Ask:

  • "Have you cleaned for Airbnb where checkout is 10am and checkin is 3pm?"
  • "Are you available Thursdays and Sundays consistently?"
  • "Do you have a modern smartphone with a working camera for 5-photo upload?"
STEP 2 (VISUAL) Spot The Flaw

The Visual Test & Damage Trap

Show candidate 3 room photos on your phone or laptop:

  • Test A: Photo of bed with loose fitted sheet & crooked pillows. Ask: "What needs fixing here?"
  • Test B (The AirCover Trap): "You walk into the living room and see a red wine stain on the rug and broken glass. What's the very first thing you do?"
  • Passing Answer: "I STOP. I do not touch or clean it. I take 3 photos (wide, medium, close-up) and text management immediately."
STEP 3 (GAUNTLET) 5-Turn Trial

The 5-Turn Probationary Period

Never sign a long-term agreement until 5 turns are completed:

  • Turn 1: Paid shadow turn with Justin or Ops Lead (in-person calibration).
  • Turns 2 & 3: Solo turns with 100% photo verification before payout.
  • Turns 4 & 5: 15-minute surprise physical inspection at 2:45 PM.
  • Graduation: Promote to Scenic Preferred Vendor with guaranteed volume.
