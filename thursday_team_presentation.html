2. Owner Agreement & SLA SOP

OpenSign Agreement Generator
Contract Gating
Tiered Service Architecture:
  • 25% Turnkey Co-Hosting (Standard): Complete hands-off management, 24/7 guest care (<3 min SLA), dynamic revenue pricing, reSeTR field turnover dispatch, linen inventory buffer, and smart lock automation.
  • 20% Hybrid Operations: Multi-channel distribution, guest messaging, and dynamic pricing. Owner coordinates their own cleaning and on-site maintenance.
  • 15% Distribution & Guest Ops (Boutique Hotels): Airbnb/VRBO channel management, Google/Meta digital ad management, and remote night desk (9:00 PM to 8:00 AM). On-site hotel staff handles day operations.
Lower Tier Exclusions Disclosure:

When an owner negotiates below 25%, the contract explicitly attaches a Schedule of Excluded Services: no reSeTR turnover dispatch, no linen restocking buffer, and no agency liability for on-site property damage.

Open-Source E-Signature Policy:

Agreements dispatch exclusively via open-source OpenSign / Documenso. We do not pay for DocuSign. Uncontacted leads in "Discovery" are locked: buttons for agreements remain hidden until advanced to closer cadence.

4. reSeTR Field Operations SOP

Housekeeping & Inspection QA
100% Photo Proof
Standardized Compensation & Roles:
  • Operative Rate: Flat $50.00/hr for certified turnovers. Payouts sent next-day via Venmo, PayPal, or Direct ACH.
  • Setup Bounty: $100 initial setup bounty per new property onboarded.
  • Cleaners as Certified Inspectors: Operatives are trained property inspectors checking linens, smart locks, water valves, and safety sensors on every turn.
Turnover Windows & 25% Inventory Buffer:

Standard cleaning window: 11:15 AM to 4:45 PM (standard 11:00 AM check-out and 5:00 PM check-in window). Every property maintains a mandatory 25% inventory buffer for linens, towels, and essential amenities locked in owner closets.

Primary Live QA Field Testers:

1545 Scenic Ave (BKL-STU-001/002/003) and 3007 Acton St (BKL-ADU-001/002/003) serve as our canonical live QA test properties. All new checklist protocols and photo verification gates must pass here first before portfolio-wide deployment.
