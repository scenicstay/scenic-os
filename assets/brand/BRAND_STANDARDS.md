## 3. Canonical Redwood Tree Icon Silhouette
The canonical redwood icon possesses distinct morphological characteristics that distinguish it from generic clip-art:
1. **Asymmetric Organic Canopy:** Natural, dense needle clusters with realistic negative-space interior notches.
2. **Slender Tapered Trunk:** Graceful vertical trunk that does not bulge awkwardly.
3. **Flared Pedestal Base:** The trunk gently and smoothly flares at the baseline (width ~36px), creating an architectural pedestal that anchors squarely onto surfaces or divider rules.
4. **Zero Edge Artifacts:** 100% clean alpha transparency with zero trace strokes, zero halos, and zero baked-in faux checkerboards.

## 4. Master Brand Lockup & Typography Rules
The horizontal brand lockup consists of four harmonized elements:
1. **Wordmark:** `SCENIC STAY` in elegant Roman capitals with classical serifs. Kerning is natural, balanced, and identical between words (resolving the previous issue where `SCENIC` was compressed and `STAY` was stretched).
2. **Centerpiece:** The Canonical Golden Redwood Tree positioned between `SCENIC` and `STAY`.
3. **Divider Rule with Lens Flare:** A tapered horizontal rule spanning the width of the mark with an incandescent flare point directly beneath the tree trunk.
4. **Subtitle:** `A BETTER BNB` in matching warm gold Roman serif small-caps with generous, elegant tracking.
