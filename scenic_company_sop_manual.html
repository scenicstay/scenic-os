The Scenic Stay Hospitality & Partner Communications Rulebook

At Scenic Stay, hospitality is not a script. It is the discipline of adding value first, answering the unspoken question behind every question, and treating guests, property owners, and field partners with warmth, fairness, and clear boundaries.

Section 01 • The Four Foundational Pillars

1. How Scenic Stay Approaches Every Interaction

1. Always Put Adding Value in Front

Whether communicating with a guest, a homeowner, a boutique hotel general manager, or a local cleaner and handyman, lead by being genuinely useful. Solve the problem or provide the local insight before asking for anything in return.

2. Answer the Question Beyond the Question (QBQ)

"Going the extra mile" is a tired cliche. True hospitality means recognizing what a person actually needs behind their literal question and providing that answer before they even have to ask. That is how we create customers and partners for life.

3. Tactful Kindness + Firm Boundaries

Maintain a warm, respectful tone while setting clear, firm expectations. Never equate kindness with weakness, and never equate meanness with strength. We pursue fairness and a sensible, cost-effective approach in every decision.

4. Relate Like a Human (Zero Superficiality)

Read what the other person actually wrote. If a guest shares why they are visiting, connect to their real plans with accurate local knowledge rather than pasting a generic "Have a great stay!" template.

Section 02 • Guest Communications Protocol

2. Guest Communications: Anticipatory Care From Booking to Checkout

Every guest touchpoint is designed to eliminate uncertainty so the traveler feels completely at home:

Pre-Arrival, Street/Path Clarity & Visitor Pass Guardrail (60/Year Cap)

Two days before arrival, confirm when we should expect the guest and highlight practical arrival details so they are never surprised after dark (such as street parking rules, keeping the driveway clear, or using a phone flashlight for the two steps down from the driveway at night). Crucial Guardrail: Never proactively offer Berkeley street parking visitor passes in standard arrival messages. We are strictly limited to 60 visitor passes per year and pay for each one. Only provide a visitor pass if a guest specifically asks or genuinely requires weekday daytime parking beyond the 2-hour window.

Proactive Early Check-In Notification

Our standard turnover window runs from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The moment our cleaning partner finishes and the unit is verified spotless, we update the guest's smart lock code activation time early and send a warm note letting them know the space is ready for them at no extra charge.

What We Never Send (Talking Their Ear Off or Wrong Geography):

"You are going to have an amazing time at the Greek Theatre, as it is truly one of the best outdoor venues anywhere! The walk there takes about 20 minutes along the hillside streets, and if you want a great spot to pregame or grab a bite beforehand, make sure to pass the Berkeley Rose Garden sunset view on your walk over..."

Why this fails: First, guests will not read a long, unprompted wall of text. Second, walking from Scenic Avenue to the Greek Theatre or UC Berkeley goes south/southeast toward campus, whereas the Rose Garden is in the opposite direction to the north.

The Scenic Stay Cadence (Crisp, Brief & Friendly + Short Maps Links When Asked):

1. When the guest's booking note has nothing substantial:
"Welcome! Let me know if there's anything we can do to help during your visit."

2. When they mention a reason for their visit (e.g., "Coming for a show at the Greek"):
"The Greek is such a great venue. Welcome! Let me know if there's anything we can do to help during your visit."

3. When they ask a specific question (e.g., "Where's a good place to eat before the show?"), dig in and ALWAYS include short Google Maps links:
"There are a couple spots near the venue off Euclid like La Val's Pizza and La Burrita, though I would recommend hitting up Berkeley Social Club, East Bay Spice Co., or Jupiter downtown."

Section 03 • Field Partner & Turnover Protocol

3. Field Operations: preSeTR Advance Schedule Radar, Recommended Off-White 3-Par Linens & Two-Phase Turnover QA

Cleaner Arrival Info vs. Guest Arrival Info & General Property Supplies

Crucial Operational Rule: A cleaner's arrival and access instructions are not always the same as a guest's check-in instructions. In HOSt Property Forge, preSeTR, and reSeTR, we maintain separate fields for Guest Arrival (street parking rules, guest path, temporary PIN) vs. Cleaner Service Access (master keypad code, lockbox backup, supply closet location, linen storage, and general property supplies & consumables restock inventory including bathroom supplies, paper goods, kitchen supplies, soaps, and unit hospitality items).

preSeTR Advance Notice + Priority Routing

Cleaners must never receive last-minute surprises. Our preSeTR cadence dispatches a 14-day lookahead, a Sunday evening weekly digest, a 48-hour confirmation, and an 8:30 AM day-of packet in each partner's preferred language (such as English, Spanish, or Brazilian Portuguese, with more languages added as we partner with teams from or in other countries). When two checkouts fall on the same day, preSeTR explicitly flags Priority 1 (Same-Day Back-to-Back 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM) vs. Priority 2 (Flexible Window with no same-day check-in).

Recommended Off-White Room-Matching 3-Par Linens + Matching Duvet Covers

Based on ten years of hosting experience, we recommend 3 full sets of off-white linens per bed (3-par rotation) plus matching duvet covers that complement each room's palette, rather than all-white linens that show stains easily and require costly frequent replacement. Having 3 sets per bed ensures cleaners can strip, remake, and rotate without same-day laundry bottlenecks, though partner homeowners may always choose to select or manage their own linens.

Two-Phase Turnover QA & Timely Partner Payouts

Inside reSeTR (accessible via clean property slugs such as resetr.abetterbnb.com/32ndstreet), cleaners follow a two-phase protocol: Phase 1 is logging any arrival damage or missing items before cleaning begins, and Phase 2 is uploading staging photos once the unit is spotless and supplies are restocked. Because we always put adding value in front for our field partners too, approved turnovers are paid out on a timely basis in our partners' preferred method of payment.

Section 04 • Homeowner & Boutique Partner Protocol

4. Owner Communications: Plain-Language Transparency & Tactful, Selective Engagement

Communicating with property owners requires a thoughtful approach: we protect their peace of mind by handling day-to-day operational noise ourselves while communicating openly, warmly, and selectively when a real decision is needed.

Calendar Stewardship & Personal Stays

We manage the master calendar across all service tiers. When an owner wants unbooked dates for personal use, we block the calendar on the owner's behalf. Both owner and manager recognize that personal blocks represent a shared revenue loss for both parties, and neither party ever cancels on confirmed guests. Following an owner stay, we coordinate the turnover cleaners (11:00 AM to 5:00 PM) and invoice the owner at cost.

Plain-Language Economics & Tactful, Selective Owner Engagement

Our agreements and statements use clear, Plain-Language terms: routine cleaning fees follow the clear pass-through structure of each owner's agreement, out-of-scope coordination (repairs, maintenance dispatches, mailing lost guest items) carries a transparent 20% coordination markup, and unexpended Resolution Center recoveries split 50/50. We practice tactful, selective owner engagement: shielding owners from minor day-to-day operational trivia while engaging them warmly, transparently, and respectfully whenever asset stewardship or policy decisions arise.

Where Things Slip or Go Wrong (And the SOP Guardrails That Fix Them)

Slip 1: Late Cleaner Advance Notice ("The Cleaner Bottleneck")

What Happens: Notifying an assigned cleaning partner too late in the game causes rushed 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM turnovers, stress on multi-turnover days, delayed early check-ins, or missed staging details.

SOP Fix: Mandatory preSeTR 14-day + 7-day + 48-hour automated advance schedule dispatch in each cleaning team's preferred language, explicitly separating Priority 1 Same-Day B2B turns from Priority 2 Flexible turns.

Slip 2: Expectations Gap on Vintage / Garden Studios ("The 'Simple' Review Trap")

What Happens: When a guest books an affordable, cozy Berkeley hillside studio expecting brand-new luxury hotel marble and receives zero pre-arrival expectation framing or Morning-1 check-in, they leave a lukewarm "simple / basic" review.

SOP Fix: Frame the peaceful garden character, super comfortable bed, and stocked welcome supplies warmly before arrival, and send a brief Morning-1 check-in so any minor need (extra blanket, heater tip) is solved in-stay.

Slip 3: Confusing Guest Arrival Info with Cleaner Service Access Info

What Happens: Sending guest check-in blurbs to a cleaner (or vice versa) leaves cleaners without supply closet codes, linen storage locations, general property supplies inventory, or clarity on whether a new guest checks in at 5:00 PM today.

SOP Fix: Strict schema and UI separation of Cleaner Service Access & Property Supplies Closet Info vs. Guest Arrival Info across HOSt Property Forge, preSeTR, and reSeTR.

Common Ways to Improve Revenue: Packaging Optional Guest Extras & Experiences

1. Guaranteed Early Check-In & Late Check-Out Tiers

While we gladly notify guests for free when a room happens to finish early on a light day, travelers who want a guaranteed early check-in (locking in 12:00 PM or 1:00 PM arrival) or a guaranteed late check-out (extending departure from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM or 2:00 PM) can purchase a priority turnover upgrade when calendar spacing allows.

2. Curated Experiences & Celebration Add-Ons

Many guests visit for anniversaries, graduations, concerts at the Greek Theatre, or wine-country getaways. Offering optional pre-arrival celebration packages (local wine or small-batch bourbon, fresh flowers, artisanal chocolates), curated local experiences, or optional mid-stay refresh cleanings turns special occasions into ancillary revenue.

3. Gap-Night ("Orphan Night") Extensions

Whenever a 1-night unbooked gap sits immediately before a guest's check-in or after their check-out, our messaging workflow can automatically offer the guest a discounted "arrive a night early" or "stay an extra night" extension, monetizing nights that would otherwise sit empty.
