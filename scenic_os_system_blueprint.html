Section 02 • Automated Handshakes Between Systems

2. The Four Core Data Loops That Run Daily Operations

To see how the pieces fit together in practice, here are the four automated handshakes that connect sales, onboarding, pricing, guest messaging, cleaning turnovers, and accounting:

Loop A: New Property Onboarding to Instant Cleaner URL

When a lead closes in Beacon Lead Command, clicking Draft Agreement passes all property fields into the Owner Agreement Generator. Once signed, clicking Open Pre-Filled Owner Intake opens HOSt Property Forge with 80% of fields already populated. Saving the intake assigns a short slug (e.g., 32ndstreet) to Supabase, which our Cloudflare Pages wildcard rule (_redirects) immediately serves at resetr.abetterbnb.com/32ndstreet without touching DNS.

Beacon Command → Owner Agreement → HOSt Forge (?mode=owner) → resetr.abetterbnb.com/:slug

Loop B: Dynamic Pricing, Gap-Night Extensions & Paid Early/Late Windows

The Dynamic Pricing Engine reads reservation spacing from preSeTR. Whenever a 1-to-2 night unbooked "orphan gap" sits next to a confirmed reservation, it flags a discounted extension offer (e.g., 25% off) for the adjacent booked guest, capturing pure margin with zero additional turnover cleaning cost. Similarly, when preSeTR confirms a Priority 2 day (no same-day back-to-back conflict), guests can lock in a paid Guaranteed Early Check-In or Late Check-Out tier 48+ hours in advance.

preSeTR Spacing Check → Gap-Night / Priority 2 Match → Crisp Guest Offer → balanSeTR Revenue Split

Loop C: Advance Cleaner Dispatch to Proactive Guest Early Check-In

preSeTR dispatches the 14-day lookahead, Sunday weekly digest, 48-hour confirmation, and 8:30 AM day-of link in each cleaning partner's preferred language, always putting Priority 1 Same-Day B2B (11:00 AM to 5:00 PM) first. When the cleaner submits Phase 2 staging photos and supply verification in reSeTR at 2:15 PM, we activate the guest's smart lock code early, send a crisp text that the unit is ready early at no extra charge, and queue a timely partner payout.

preSeTR 14d/7d/48h Alert → reSeTR Phase 1 & 2 QA → Smart Lock + Early Guest Note → Timely Partner Payout

Loop D: Payouts, Extra Coordination & Global Tax Readiness

Every booking payout, approved turnover, out-of-scope maintenance/mailing dispatch (+20% coordination markup), post-owner-stay cleaning invoice, and 50/50 unexpended Resolution Center recovery flows into balanSeTR. Tabs 3 and 4 automatically generate plain-language monthly owner statements and year-end tax packages across domestic and international jurisdictions for both our owners and our company.

OTA + reSeTR Feeds → balanSeTR Auto-Rules → Monthly Owner Statement → Year-End Tax Package

Section 05 • Beacon Scout Active Seeker Mechanics & East Bay Priority

5. How Beacon Scout Evaluates Listings With GLiNER, Verifies They Are Above Board, and Radiates Outward From Berkeley

Does Beacon Scout have to run heavy AI across every listing in a city? No. To keep compute cost at $0.00 and scan speed at 4 to 8 seconds per ZIP code, Beacon Scout uses a 3-Stage Sieve paired with a Concentric East Bay ZIP Schedule starting right in Berkeley where our cleaning partners already operate:

Pass 01 • < 2ms Metadata Sieve

Instant Header Filter ($0 Cost)

Checks already-listed properties at the metadata level first: filters out large commercial PM conglomerates (> 5 listings) and isolates independent hosts (1 to 3 listings) that show either review friction (< 4.82★) or uncaptured ADR/occupancy promise. Eliminates ~75% of noise in milliseconds before GLiNER ever runs.

Pass 02 • ~25ms Local GLiNER + Rules

4-Dimension Multi-Criteria Scoring

Our local GLiNER span extractor + deterministic rule engine evaluates surviving listings simultaneously across 4 criteria: (1) High-Value Asset Traits (0-25 pts: views, ADU/garden studio, Craftsman charm, campus/transit proximity), (2) Operational Distress (0-30 pts: check-in, cleaning, or comms slips), (3) Promise & Yield Upside (0-25 pts: static pricing on Greek Theatre/Cal weekends, orphan gaps), and (4) Cleaner Coverage Proximity (0-20 pts).

Pass 03 • Compliance Gate

"Above-Board" Permit & Deed Check

Before any lead enters Beacon Lead Command, we double-check that the already-listed property is 100% above board: verifying an active displayed municipal permit/TOT registration (such as a City of Berkeley ZCSTR permit on a hosted cottage/ADU) and matching the owner's grant deed on the Alameda or Contra Costa County Assessor roll.

Why We Scan by ZIP Code in Concentric Rings From Berkeley (Ring 1 Cleaner Corridor First)

Searching a whole city or region at once hits OTA pagination caps (~270 listings) and wastes time on areas where we do not yet have cleaners. Scanning by ZIP code radiating outward from 1545 Scenic Ave guarantees complete coverage where our East Bay cleaning partners can service properties immediately:
• Ring 1A (Berkeley Core): 94708 (Berkeley Hills), 94709 (Northside), 94705 (Claremont/Elmwood), 94703, 94702 (West Berkeley / Acton).
• Ring 1B (Oakland & Emeryville): 94618 (Rockridge), 94611 (Montclair / Estates Dr Hills), 94609 (Temescal), 94608 (32nd St / Emeryville).
• Ring 1C (I-80 North Corridor): 94706 (Albany), 94530 (Kensington / El Cerrito), 94801 (Point Richmond), 94564 (Pinole / Avis Way).

Cadence & Compute Cost: 1 ZIP Slice Every 15 Minutes ($0.00 Local Compute)

Because Pass 1, Pass 2 (local gliner_small-v2.1 with RAM guard), and Pass 3 run locally on your machine without paid LLM tokens, scanning one ZIP code slice (50 to 100 listings) takes only 4 to 8 seconds and costs $0.00. Running one ZIP slice every 15 minutes cycles through all 13 Ring 1 East Bay ZIPs every ~3 hours while simultaneously ingesting Claude's daily boutique & destination search files and applying a +20-point boost to newly discovered, uncontacted leads.
