## SECTION 1: MASTER CO-HOSTING, DIRECT BOOKING, AND RESOLUTION CENTER TERMS

### 1.1 Direct Bookings & Agentic Marketplace Channel Fee (+6.0% Channel Fee)
* **Base Management Commission Consistency:** Direct bookings (via `abetterbnb.com`) and Agentic Marketplace reservations carry the **exact same base management commission percentage** as the property's selected contract tier (15.0%, 20.0%, or 25.0%), with zero commission discount.
* **Additional 6.0% Direct & Agentic Channel Fee:** An additional **six percent (6.0%)** Direct & Agentic Channel Fee applies to direct and agentic bookings. This covers the ~3.0% merchant credit card processing/interchange fee plus 3.0% for direct booking engine infrastructure, identity verification, and channel distribution costs that Scenic Stay does not absorb. Even with the 6.0% channel fee, the total cost to the booking is far lower than Airbnb's 14.0% to 15.5% host/guest OTA service fees.
* **Mandatory Guest Damage Protection Waiver:** Because direct and agentic bookings operate outside Airbnb AirCover, every direct reservation includes a mandatory **Guest Damage Protection Waiver fee** (charged at the prevailing commercial rate at the time of stay and folded directly into the guest stay cost), with formal Owner sign-off.

### 1.2 Resolution Center Claims & Mandatory Invoice Verification Rule (50% Split)
1. **50% Claim Split Absent Verified Paid Invoices:** Whenever Scenic Stay investigates, documents, files, or negotiates a Resolution Center claim, guest damage dispute, or extra cleaning fee recovery, **Scenic Stay retains fifty percent (50.0%) of the gross payout UNLESS Owner provides a verified, paid third-party invoice or receipt proving actual out-of-pocket replacement or repair** (or proof that 100% of an extra cleaning claim payout was actually paid out to the cleaners).
2. **No Windfall Retention Without Replacement or Cleaner Payout:**
   * *Extra Cleaning Claim Example ($250.00 Claim):* If a guest leaves a unit excessively dirty and a $250.00 extra cleaning claim is collected, that payout exists to compensate the cleaning crew for extra labor and to compensate Scenic Stay for prosecuting the claim. An owner cannot pocket 100% of a $250.00 extra cleaning payout if the cleaners were not paid that extra $250.00.
   * *Children's Room Blanket/Comforter & Minor Furniture Blemishes:* Filing a claim for a stained children's comforter where Owner has no intention of replacing the item (or where the 14-day Airbnb filing window has long expired) is an unproductive use of administrative time and risks retaliatory 1-star guest reviews. Without a verified replacement receipt, any approved claim recovery is split 50% to Manager and 50% to Owner.
