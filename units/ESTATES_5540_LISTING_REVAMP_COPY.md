## 7. HOUSE RULES & GOOD NEIGHBOR COVENANT

We take immense pride in preserving the peace and serenity of this quiet hillside canyon community. All guests must agree to our Good Neighbor Covenant prior to entry:

1. **Quiet Hours:** 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM daily. Sound carries easily in the canyon. Exterior decks and outdoor conversation must move indoors by 10:00 PM.
2. **Strict No-Party Policy:** No unauthorized gatherings, weddings, commercial video productions, or outside parties of any kind. Unregistered visitors beyond confirmed reservation count are strictly prohibited.
3. **Smoke-Free Property:** Zero smoking or vaping of any kind (tobacco, cannabis, or e-cigarettes) anywhere on the premises, including all decks, balconies, and driveway. Violations incur a $500 remediation fine plus specialized sanitization costs.
4. **Wildlife & Fire Safety:** The Montclair hills are an active wildfire zone and natural wildlife habitat (deer, owls, wild turkeys). No open wood fires, fireworks, or charcoal grills. Trash must remain secured inside bear-resistant receptacles.
5. **Maximum Occupancy:** Maximum overnight occupancy is 10 guests.
